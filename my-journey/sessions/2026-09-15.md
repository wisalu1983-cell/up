## 2026-09-15｜午间按摩与同事沟通

### 当次学习

- `during lunch`
- `I’m getting a massage.`
- `Feel free to contact me.`
- `If anything’s urgent, feel free to ping me.`
- `pick one near the office`

### 延迟复现证据

提示后曾正确说出：

> I’m getting a massage during lunch.  
> If anything’s urgent, feel free to ping me.

几分钟后自由复述重新出现：

> I’m getting to have a massage.  
> in the lunch time  
> if anything urgent

### 结论

该次训练明确证明：当场跟读或提示后正确，尚未等于主动掌握。后续必须进行当场、约 5 分钟后、第二天和数天后的分层复测。
