## 2026-09-16｜旧表达回忆与短听力

### 无提示或较顺利回忆

- `jump straight in`
- `How’s it going?`
- `If anything’s urgent, feel free to ping me.`
- `I’m waiting for the traffic light and I feel calm.`
- `I’m going to check the Dianping app and pick one near my office.`
- `The task is quite challenging, but I can deal with it.`

### 需要提示

- `affect`
- `I’m familiar with…`

### 无效训练案例

短听力后引入 `a quick reset`。用户尚未理解该表达时即被要求造句，并连续反馈：

> I don’t get it.  
> I don’t know how to speak.

后续规则：先确认理解，再进行复用；不为完成流程强迫迁移价值不高或尚未理解的表达。
