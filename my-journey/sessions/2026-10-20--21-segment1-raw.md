## 本次实际使用的训练要求（学生原消息）

Christina, please run Segment 1 using this message as your complete lesson script.

GOAL
Help me make clear requests and explain what I plan to do. Aim for 12–15 minutes of active practice.

TEACHING RULES
- Speak English at a natural, slightly slower pace.
- Follow Tasks 1–4 in order. Use the opening situations below as written.
- Give me one action at a time and wait for my answer.
- If I ask for context, repeat or explain the facts of the SAME situation. Keep its purpose unchanged.
- Let me finish speaking. Respond to my meaning first.
- When useful, give ONE specific improvement: identify my wording, explain the change briefly, and offer a simple natural version. Then let me try once.
- Accept natural alternatives. Keep small grammar corrections brief. An already clear answer does not need compulsory repetition.
- Use simple questions to check understanding.
- Keep track of completed and unfinished tasks yourself.

TASK 1 — A real travel habit, about 2 minutes

Ask:
“When you travel, how do you usually find a restaurant you want to visit?”

After my answer, ask for one real example. If I cannot remember an example, ask why I prefer that method.

Give brief feedback on what I communicated.

TASK 2 — Finding the hotel restaurant, about 4 minutes

Say:
“You arrived at the hotel yesterday. You will stay for two more nights. This morning, you want breakfast, but you don’t know where the restaurant is. I’m the receptionist. Ask me to help you find it.”

Wait for my request before giving any model sentence.

Respond as the receptionist:
“The restaurant is beside the garden. I can take you there now.”

Let me respond to that offer.

Then give one useful improvement if needed and let me retry once.

Completion requires my request AND my response to your offer. Keep this task about finding the breakfast restaurant.

TASK 3 — Going to hear a talk, about 3 minutes

Say:
“You are a game producer visiting GDC to learn about AI games. You have already registered and are standing in the lobby. There is a talk about AI games in Room C. You want to hear the speaker. I’m your colleague. Tell me what you plan to do next and why, in one or two short sentences.”

Let me answer first.

If I struggle to express hearing the talk, directly teach:
“listen to the talk”

A possible simple sentence is:
“I’ll go to Room C and listen to the talk.”

Explain the meaning simply, confirm that it matches my intention, and let me express my plan again in my own words.

If I already use a natural alternative such as “attend the talk,” accept it. Offer the phrase briefly without treating my original answer as a failure.

TASK 4 — A new request situation, about 3 minutes

Clearly introduce this as a SEPARATE role-play.

Say:
“You have just arrived at a conference and need to collect your entry pass. You can’t find the registration desk, where people collect their passes. I’m a volunteer. Ask me to help you find it.”

Wait for my request. Do not supply a request sentence beforehand.

Respond as the volunteer:
“The registration desk is straight ahead. I can show you.”

Let me respond. Give specific feedback if useful.

Completion requires a new request AND a response to the volunteer.

ENDING — About 1 minute

Give:
1. One specific effective thing I did.
2. One useful focus for next time.

Check Tasks 1–4 against my actual answers. State any unfinished actions accurately. Repeating your model is supported practice, not independent mastery.

Do not begin Segment 2. Save the breakpoint and the phrases or models you provided.

If I pause, keep the exact unfinished action. When I return, resume there without restarting completed tasks.

Start now with the question in Task 1.
